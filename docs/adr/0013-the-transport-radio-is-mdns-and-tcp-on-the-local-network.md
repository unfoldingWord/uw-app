# The Transport radio is mDNS and TCP on the shared local network

SH-1 and SH-2 need phone-to-phone transfer between iOS and Android both ways with no internet. No Expo module offers a radio that crosses the two platforms, and AGENTS.md section 3 left Transport open until a proposal picked one. Issue #2 approved `docs/proposals/2026-09-29-transport-radio.md`: the phones meet on a shared local network (a hotspot one of them opens, or any Wi-Fi without internet), find each other by mDNS, and move bytes over TCP. The platform adapter is `react-native-tcp-socket` for a length-prefixed stream link (`src/platform/stream-link.ts`, tested in Node) and a local Expo module, `modules/uw-radio/`, for mDNS through Android `NsdManager` and iOS `dns_sd` (`DNSServiceRegister`, `DNSServiceBrowse`, `DNSServiceResolve` and `DNSServiceGetAddrInfo`, which resolve an address without opening a connection; `NetService` until issue #64), the local IPv4 address, the Android app package and the installer hand-off. The receiver proves the pairing code in its hello and the sender declines a wrong one; when discovery finds nothing, the address is typed or scanned from a QR code the system camera opens as an `unfoldingword://transfer` link, so no camera permission is asked. The Transport port gained `Advertisement.address`, `AppPackage.source` and `install(path)`. Rejected in the proposal: Bluetooth for the payload (tens of kB/s across the platforms, ten minutes or more for 60 MB, and location to scan on Android 11 and earlier), Multipeer Connectivity (iOS only), the Nearby Connections wrappers (Nearby on Android and Multipeer on iOS do not talk to each other, and they need Google Play services), and Wi-Fi Aware (often missing on low-end Android hardware, and new on iOS). The cost is that the leader must join both phones to one network, iOS asks for local network access, and none of it has been compiled or run on a phone: the two-phone spike in step 4 of the proposal (60 MB both ways in under two minutes, no location prompt) is still the gate, and until it is recorded SH-1 and SH-2 are proven only on the sim's memory bus.
