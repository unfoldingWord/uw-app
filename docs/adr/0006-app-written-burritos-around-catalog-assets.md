# The app writes a burrito around assets the catalog lists

ADR 0002 says content enters the app only as a burrito, taken from the archives DCS generates. Two resources the PRD requires have no such archive: the Open Bible Stories pictures (LA-3), which DCS serves from its CDN and which no catalog subject lists, and audio (ST-4, LA-4), which DCS publishes as `.m4a` release assets flagged by `attachment_types.audio`. Issue #8 approved `docs/proposals/2026-09-29-provisional-flavors.md` and answered its open question: the app may write a burrito around assets the catalog lists, and what it writes passes the same validator as a DCS burrito. The Image Pack is built from the 360px pictures the `unfoldingWord/en_obs` release cites (`https://cdn.door43.org/obs/jpg/360px/obs-en-NN-NN.jpg`), with that release's provenance and licence, as `peripheral/x-obsImages` in language `zxx`. An Audio Pack is built from a release's audio assets, with that release's provenance and the `LICENSE.md` at its tag, as `scripture/audioTranslation`, keyed by story (`OBS: [n]`) for Open Bible Stories and by book and chapter for a Bible, with `audio/mp4` admitted. The burrito is written into the install's staging directory, verified and renamed into place like any other, so atomic replacement, checksums and events stay one path (ADR 0005). This is still not a converter: nothing is parsed from a Resource Container, the app only lists files it downloaded and describes them in the standard's metadata. The cost is that a picture or clip the CDN or DCS removes is a failed install rather than a validation error upstream, and that the licence of an audio release is read from its repository rather than from a burrito DCS signed off. Formation stays a provisional row until DCS generates its archive.
